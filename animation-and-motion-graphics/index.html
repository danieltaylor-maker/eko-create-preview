How we work

From brief to delivery

  1. Brief

    The message, the audience and where the film will be seen.

  2. Script

    A clear script and voiceover, agreed before any design work.

  3. Design

    Style frames and storyboards that show how it will look.

  4. Animate

    Animation, sound and voiceover brought together, with clear rounds of feedback.

  5. Deliver

    Final files in every format and size you need.

Who we work with
